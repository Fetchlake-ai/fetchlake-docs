title: "Connectors"
description: "Bring PDFs in from Zotero or Google Drive and keep them in sync."

Connectors copy PDFs from somewhere you already keep them into a Fetchlake collection. They're in **Dashboard → Tools → Connectors** and need a paid plan.

## Zotero

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Zotero API key">
    In Zotero, go to **Settings → Security → Applications** and create a key. It needs **read/write** access, because Fetchlake writes references back to your Zotero library when you add papers from other sources. Include group libraries if you need them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the credential">
    In **Connectors**, choose **New Zotero credential** and enter your **Library ID**, the **Library type** (personal or group) and the key. The label is just for you, for example "Thesis library".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync into a collection">
    Open the collection and choose a Zotero collection to sync. Fetchlake pulls in the PDFs attached to its items and processes them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Leave the key field blank when editing a credential to keep the existing key.

## Google Drive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Drive">
    Choose **Connect Drive** and sign in with Google. Fetchlake only asks for read-only access to your Drive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a folder">
    From a collection, choose the Drive folder to sync. The PDFs in it are copied in and processed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If a sync stops working, use **Re-authorise** to refresh the Google sign-in.

## Tips

- Sync a single Zotero collection or Drive folder per Fetchlake collection, so the two stay matched.
- Your credentials are stored encrypted and are never shown again after you save them.
- Items without a PDF come in as **Reference only**. Attach the file in Zotero and sync again.

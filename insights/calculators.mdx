Find them at **fetchlake.com/insights/calculators**. They run entirely in your browser.

## Position size

**How many shares to buy so that hitting your stop loses only the amount you chose, costs included.**

Enter your **account size**, **risk per trade**, **entry price**, **stop price** and brokerage. It returns the **shares to buy**, the **position value** and the **loss if stopped out**.

*Use it when:* you've decided where you'd exit a losing trade and want the loss capped at, say, 1% of your portfolio.

## Brokerage and break-even

**What a trade costs both ways, and how far the price must rise before you're back to even.**

Enter the buy price, number of shares and brokerage each way. It shows the **round-trip cost** and the **break-even sell price**.

*Use it when:* making small trades. A $10 brokerage each way on a $1,000 trade means the price has to rise 2% just to break even.

## CGT 12-month discount

**Tax on a share sale before and after the 12-month mark, and the first day the discount applies.**

Australian residents who hold shares for more than 12 months usually get a 50% discount on the capital gain. Enter the cost base, sale proceeds, buy and sell dates, your marginal tax rate and any capital losses. It shows the tax if sold before and after 12 months, and how much waiting saves.

*Use it when:* you're thinking of selling a share you've held for nearly a year.

## Compound growth

**Growth from regular contributions, with dividends reinvested or taken as cash.**

Enter a starting amount, monthly contributions, price growth, dividend yield, fees and years. It shows what you put in, the growth on your money, and the extra gained by reinvesting dividends.

*Use it when:* comparing a dividend reinvestment plan with taking the income, or setting a savings target.

<Note>
The calculators are estimates for education. Tax depends on your own situation, so check with a registered tax agent or adviser before acting.
</Note>

Fetchlake Insights tests popular investing ideas against decades of ASX price data and company accounts, and publishes what it finds, whether the idea works or not. It also has free calculators for the sums every investor has to do.

Find it at **fetchlake.com/insights**.

<Note>
Insights is general research and education, not financial advice. Nothing on it is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
</Note>

## Three promises

1. **Every idea is written down and committed before it is tested.** The rules, time periods and pass mark are dated in advance.
2. **Trading costs are counted**, so small edges don't look bigger than they are.
3. **Failures are published** alongside anything that works.

## Getting the most out of it

- **Read the "how we tested it" section**, not just the verdict. It tells you exactly what was and wasn't tested.
- **A "Rejected" verdict is useful.** It means a popular rule didn't beat its costs on the ASX in the years tested, which may save you from trading it.
- **Use the calculators before you trade**, especially position size and break-even. They show what costs do to small trades.

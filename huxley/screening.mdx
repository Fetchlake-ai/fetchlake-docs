The screening view shows one article at a time (title, authors, journal, year and abstract) with your criteria keywords highlighted. The list on the side shows each record's current decision.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Y</kbd> | Include |
| <kbd>N</kbd> | Exclude |
| <kbd>M</kbd> | Maybe |
| <kbd>J</kbd> | Next article |
| <kbd>K</kbd> | Previous article |

Shortcuts don't work while the cursor is in a text box. Click on the article to use them again.

## Filtering the list

Narrow the list to work in batches:

- **Title keywords** and **phrase search**
- **Boolean query** (`AND`, `OR`, `NOT`, brackets)
- **Year range** (from year)
- **Journals** (hold Ctrl/Cmd to choose several)
- **Decision**: pending, accepted, rejected, maybe

Filters also set the scope of an AI run if you choose **Screen current filtered records**. See [AI screening](/huxley/ai-screening).

## Tips

- Screen in short sessions. Accuracy drops after about an hour.
- Use filters to clear obvious exclusions in bulk (for example, animal studies) before screening the rest one by one.
- When unsure, press <kbd>M</kbd> and move on. Don't stall on one record.
